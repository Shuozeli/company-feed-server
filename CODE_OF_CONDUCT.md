# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous project.
Contributors should be constructive, assume good intent, welcome different
levels of experience, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the
[repository security reporting channel](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions, limit participation, or ban participants when behavior
threatens a safe and productive community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
person who reports a concern is prohibited.
