# Content rights and provenance

The schemas, manifests, validation script, and project-authored documentation
in this repository are available under the terms in `LICENSE.md`.

Article text, headlines, company names, trademarks, and linked publisher assets
remain subject to the rights of their respective owners. They are not
relicensed by this repository. Every record retains its canonical publisher URL
and source provenance.

## Current publication scope

The current export target uses the explicit `approved_public` publication
scope. Under this scope, the exporter can include non-private items from
approved, currently valid RSS, Atom, HTML, and browser sources even when the
source-level `public_export_allowed` flag is false.

“Approved” is an operator quality and source-status decision for the collection
pipeline. It is not a copyright license, permission to redistribute, publisher
endorsement, or representation that material is free of third-party rights.
Likewise, public accessibility, an official company domain, and source
ownership signals do not by themselves grant redistribution rights.

Consumers are responsible for evaluating the original publisher's terms and
the rights that apply to their intended use. The CC0 waiver in `LICENSE.md`
does not apply to the third-party corpus.

## Public correction and rights routes

Use the public
[data-correction form](https://github.com/Shuozeli/company-news-data/issues/new?template=data-correction.yml)
for an incorrect company identity or category, canonical URL, title, date,
provenance field, duplicate, or extracted body.

Use the public
[rights and removal form](https://github.com/Shuozeli/company-news-data/issues/new?template=rights.yml)
for copyright, licensing, permission, attribution, trademark, or removal
concerns. Include the repository paths and original publisher URLs needed to
identify the material, but do not post confidential or unnecessary personal
information in a public issue.

Requests may result in a source correction, a future-snapshot exclusion, a
replacement of extracted text with metadata and a canonical link, or another
appropriate repository action. Git history is retained by default; a history
rewrite can be considered when a rights or privacy issue requires removal from
earlier snapshots.
