# Content rights and provenance

The schemas, manifests, validation script, and project-authored documentation
in this repository are available under the terms in `LICENSE.md`.

Article text, headlines, company names, trademarks, and linked publisher assets
remain subject to the rights of their respective owners. They are not
relicensed by this repository. Every record retains its canonical publisher URL
and source provenance.

The exporter includes only non-private records from approved sources for which
the operator explicitly set `public_export_allowed=true`. Public accessibility
alone is not treated as redistribution permission.

Requests to correct provenance, remove material from future snapshots, or
address a rights concern should be filed through the contact process documented
by the repository owner. Git history is immutable by default; legally required
history rewrites are handled as exceptional maintenance.
